Free Legal Aid Eligibility (NALSA)

Under Article 39A of the Indian Constitution, you are legally entitled to 100% FREE lawyer representation if you are:

  • Women and Children
  • Members of SC / ST Communities
  • Persons in Police Custody or Jail
  • Annual income below ₹3 Lakhs
  • Industrial workers & disaster victims
How to avail: Visit the DLSA office at your District Court or call toll-free 15100.
